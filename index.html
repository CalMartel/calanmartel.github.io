About

I'm a materials engineer focused on electrochemical energy storage — the science of how we capture, hold, and release energy at scale. I grew up in the Sierra Nevada foothills, and the mountains shaped how I think: carefully, with respect for what's already there.

I earned my MEng at UC Berkeley's Fung Institute, concentrating in Electrochemical Materials Engineering. Before that, I spent two years as a battery engineer at NexTech Batteries, building hands-on experience with lithium-sulfur cell development, test infrastructure, and failure analysis.

I'm about to join Green Grid Energy Storage to work on liquid salt battery systems for the grid — a technology I believe has real potential to change how we store renewable energy at scale. I'm also currently taking an electrochemical battery device course through UC Berkeley to deepen the technical foundation I'll need for this work.
